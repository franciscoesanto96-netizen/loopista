Loopista – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. Draft prepared with an AI assistant: have it reviewed before you publish, it is not legal advice.

Who we are

Loopista is a mobile game published by Francisco Santo (Portugal). Contact: franciscoesbiz@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (stars, coins, energy, upgrades, skins, best times) and your options (language, sound, vibration, speed units) are saved only on your device. There are no accounts and no chat.

Purchases and usage statistics (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to process in-app purchases and to count users. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier, the app version, your device model and OS, your country, and your purchase history. It does not receive your name or email. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings > Privacy choices. See policies.google.com/privacy. Buying "Remove Ads" removes the ads.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and has no violence, chat or external links. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. REPLACE: confirm your AdMob child-directed settings (tag for child-directed treatment, maximum ad content rating) before release.

Game Center, iCloud and notifications

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game uses your Game Center player ID to show leaderboards and events (we never see your name or e-mail). If iCloud is on, your progress (levels, stars, coins, upgrades) is saved in your private iCloud storage so it follows you to your other devices; you can switch this off in Settings. Reminders (daily reward, come back and play) are local notifications created on your device, only if you allow them, and you can turn them off in Settings or in iOS Settings.

Your choices

You can switch off personalised ads in your iOS settings, reset your progress in Settings, and delete the app to remove all local data. To ask about data held by our processors, contact franciscoesbiz@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
